# Global estimate: not yet evidence-backed

The initial handoff's 15,000–30,000 investors and €152,000 example are not adopted as estimates. They were hypothetical and the Czech development sample cannot validate them.

## Required estimate structure

Stratify by investor category, region, language and source transparency. Define managers versus funds, people versus organizations, direct investing versus intermediaries, and active versus historically evidenced investors. Use dated association and public directory counts with compatible definitions and an explicit overlap adjustment. State a range rather than summing incompatible directories.

For each stratum record candidate count, expected acceptance rate, sources per candidate, retrieval/API costs, research time, accepted-record review time, unresolved/excluded-record review time, QA sampling, deduplication effort and refresh rate. Measure these in additional pilots before claiming a global total.

## Cost equation

`total = discovery + collection/API + extraction + review of accepted + review of unresolved/excluded + independent QA + engineering + maintenance`

`review cost = candidate count × review probability × average minutes / 60 × hourly rate`

Use separate review probabilities and times for accepted, unresolved and excluded records. Report low/base/high assumptions and a one-year maintenance scenario. Document licensing/reuse constraints separately from technical access. Do not label a scenario calculation as a measured budget.

## What this pilot establishes

Some official sites publish ticket ranges and fund information; others do not. Multiple financial metrics and currencies exist. A small structured evidence model and static search UI are practical. It does not establish worldwide investor counts, expected precision, completeness or cost. Those conclusions remain outstanding in the assignment.
