## Decisions that need care

Miton qualifies because the criteria cover venture investment organizations, including those investing their own money. It is labeled as an own-capital investment group. Its portfolio value cannot stand in for total investable capital.

Presto's 2022 participation is confirmed by a 2024 publication. That does not make it a 2024 transaction or prove current investment capacity. Its current sector focus is separate from the older portfolio example.

Purple's base is an inference from a Brno-based description on its official site and its office details. Presto's historical Prague description is corroborated by current operational contact information. Legal-register verification remains outstanding. These judgments are visible, rather than presented as official certifications.

A similarly named business on a different domain must not be merged with Credo. The official VC domain used here is `credoventures.com`.
