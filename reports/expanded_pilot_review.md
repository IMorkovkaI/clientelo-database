This is recorded in the capital note as a portfolio-company count, without inventing a financial meaning or using it to infer focus. Its focus is already stated explicitly. The linked [sub-fund statute](https://jsk-funds.cdn-stoone.co/jsk-funds/JSK%20SICAV_statut-podfond_VC_20260826-1.pdf), effective 26 August 2026, identifies JSK Investments SICAV a.s., IČO 23823445, its Prague registered office, and conditional stage criteria. This supports Czech legal domicile from an issuer document. The live register and regulator listing were not independently retrieved. The group contact company has a different IČO and must not be substituted for the SICAV.

[Miton's portfolio](https://www.miton.cz/en/portfolio/) supports B2B, software and marketplace observations through named company descriptions. [Purple's portfolio](https://www.purple-ventures.com/) supports B2B/software observations through Bitreport and Sloneek. These are marked as portfolio examples.
