# Investor Atlas

## Register
product

## Users
People searching for suitable Czech startup investors, and an evaluator inspecting an assignment deliverable.

## Product Purpose
A small evidence-backed research database. Search and rank investor profiles, inspect sources, build a personal Final draft, compare two or three investors, and export an ordered shortlist. The assignment also requires inclusion methodology, independent human audit, measured accuracy, and a justified global expansion estimate.

## Brand Personality
Calm, precise, useful. English interface as discussed in the chat; preserve the Czech original brief.

## Anti-references
No marketing landing page, oversized statistics, decorative card grids, unexplained AI confidence scores, or fabricated completeness.

## Design Principles
Facts before decoration. Keep evidence beside each claim. Distinguish unknowns from mismatches. The user owns the final order. Preserve context across navigation. Suitability weighting is provisional pending a later discussion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard-operable filters, visible focus, labeled controls, text alongside color, reduced motion, responsive layouts. Aim for WCAG AA contrast and test with automated accessibility checks; do not claim certification.
