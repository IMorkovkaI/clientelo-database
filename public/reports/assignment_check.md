## Limits that remain visible

The assignment allows a smaller pilot; the initial handoff's 30–50 target is not treated as a requirement of the quoted text. The pilot validates Czech VC collection only. Worldwide categories and costs require broader sampled research before implementation at scale.

The owner supplied an explicit aggregate correctness verdict after describing the reviewed fields and profiles. That supports the stated field-source-agreement measurement. It does not turn agent judgments into human entity judgments or provide an independent cell audit. The two new profiles, subsequent corrections and legal-register additions remain agent-reviewed. Unknown amounts and context omissions are reported separately from errors in supplied values.

Financial completeness remains a substantive limit: fund targets and portfolio values cannot substitute for all requested investment capital. The repository explains the gaps rather than claiming complete data. Legal corroboration covers three named Czech entities and does not establish every fund relationship or regulator authorization.

See [validation](https://github.com/IMorkovkaI/clientelo-database/blob/main/docs/VALIDATION.md) for 55 passing unit/data tests, 17 passing production E2E tests and actual saved CSV/Excel files. The GitHub workflow and deployed page remain to be checked after publication. Local results do not establish deployment success.
