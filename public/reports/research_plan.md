# Research plan and acceptance rules

Revision dated 16 October 2026. The project already had inclusion rules before this review. This consolidated revision follows the eight-profile pilot; it is not a claim that the new estimates were written before that pilot. Apply it prospectively before expanding the dataset.

1. **Define the unit.** One manager or direct-investing organization, not one record per fund. For later individual categories, one identifiable person with documented personal investments. Record aliases and parent relationships. Do not infer personal investing from employment at a fund.
2. **Find candidates.** Use public association lists and official investor/portfolio-company pages. Log every lead and discovery URL, including exclusions. Check other directories so one association does not define the market.
3. **Check identity.** Connect the official brand, legal organization and management team. Use public registers when legal relationships are ambiguous. A matching name alone is insufficient; keep ambiguity unresolved.
4. **Apply the Czech rule.** The main investment management operation must be in Czechia. An explicit Prague/Brno operating-base statement, corroborated by office/team information, can qualify. Citizenship, a mailbox, fund domicile or investing in Czech companies cannot establish that alone. Multiple offices without a clear main base remain unresolved.
5. **Check investor activity.** Require a VC strategy and a named company investment with a dated public source identifying participation. An own-capital startup investor may qualify; a fund structure is not mandatory. Exclude advisers, accelerators without investing evidence, lenders without qualifying equity/convertible activity, LP-only investors and PE-only firms from this VC pilot. Later PE and family-office strata have their own direct-company-investment criteria.
6. **Extract claims separately.** Link sector, stage, geography, ticket and capital to sources. Preserve currencies, fund scope and dates. Distinguish whole-round size from contribution, target from raised fund size, AUM from portfolio value. Do not assume a person's wealth is investment capital.
7. **Keep unknowns visible.** Use null plus not_found, not_disclosed, not_checked or conflicting. Missing financial data does not disqualify a proven investor. Missing identity, Czech base or investment proof prevents acceptance.
8. **Record evidence.** Save URL, publisher, short excerpt, explanation, publication and retrieval dates. Preserve announcement, transaction and financial as-of dates separately. A retrospective article can establish a past investment without establishing its original announcement or settlement date.
9. **Review and measure.** Freeze decisions before corrections. Check each selected record against public evidence, record reviewer type and changed fields, and compare against the frozen version. Report sample size and unresolved cases. The current sample is purposive and AI-reviewed; report agreement separately from independently established precision. See pilot_results.md. A separate owner-reported check found no incorrect populated values among 62 checked fields in the earlier 15-profile version; its scope and sampling limits are recorded in pilot_results.md.
10. **Expand only after recalibration.** Use the sourced low/middle/high estimates in global_estimate.md and expansion-calculation.md. Next test 50 candidates per specified stratum, record actual time/tool spend, then revise yield and budget. Refresh evidence regularly and label stale observations. The agent owns this work.

Acceptance means identity, Czech management, VC activity and dated participation evidence are all supported. Explicit contrary evidence means out_of_scope. Insufficient or conflicting evidence means unresolved. These decisions describe the pilot's scope, not whether someone will fund a particular company.

The app is an evidence viewer and shortlist tool. No ranking score, structural test or association membership substitutes for these checks.
