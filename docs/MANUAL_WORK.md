# Research responsibility and future work

The agent owns investor research, source checking, analysis, implementation and report generation. The owner supplied product decisions, specific sources and clarifications, and the recorded manual field check. Additional manual research is not a prerequisite assigned to the owner.

This pilot contains 17 eligible profiles and 39 candidates. The scoped human result is 62/62 populated fields with no reported error in five sections of the earlier 15-profile snapshot; 13 unknowns are separate. It does not validate later additions or establish entity precision. See [pilot results](../reports/pilot_results.md).

Future research can resolve Nation1 and Thein, investigate the remaining discovery leads, expand legal-entity matching beyond the three ARES checks, and seek missing financial disclosures. Global collection needs broader samples and measured effort to recalibrate the completed scenario estimate. These are scaling opportunities and disclosed pilot limits, not undisclosed requirements for the current sample.

The existing editable intake CSV is preserved for future investor additions. Its rows are leads until the agent checks sources, deduplicates and applies the inclusion rules. Never overwrite the intake or mix new candidates into a frozen evaluation sample.

The owner will publish this repository. E2E normally runs at publication; the owner explicitly requested an earlier run, which passed all 17 production tests. Unit/data checks and the production build also passed. See [README](../README.md) for the publication commands and [validation](VALIDATION.md) for actual results.
